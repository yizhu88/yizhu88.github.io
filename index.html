Hi, I'm Yi Zhu (朱薏). I am a first-year Master's student in Urban and Rural Planning at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Feng Zhen and Assoc. Prof. Xiao Qin. I am actively seeking PhD opportunities in human geography, health geography, and computational urban studies.

My research sits at the intersection of urban spatial inequality, mixed land use, and urban renewal. I integrate computational methods — including NLP/NER pipelines, knowledge graphs, and LLM-based multi-agent workflows — with critical spatial theory to understand how urban structure shapes social outcomes.

Before planning, I completed a year of medical coursework and participated in clinical observation, including a pediatric ICU internship — a background I now bring to health geography research on environmental inequity and mobility-based exposure assessment.

Research Interests

  • Land use mix, spatial inequality, and the mixed-use inflection point (analogous to the Environmental Kuznets Curve)
  • Mobility-based environmental health exposure assessment correcting for UGCoP bias
  • LLM-assisted inference of activity context from human mobility traces
  • Urban renewal performance evaluation via multi-agent LLM workflows & social media analysis
  • Historical industrial patterns and collective spatial memory (archival NLP)
